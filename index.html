Holy Spirit Prompts

A quiet place to write down what you sense, and to keep what God has done.

Some things arrive as a nudge rather than a plan — a name that comes to mind, a verse that lands differently, a sense that you should go. They're easy to lose by the end of the day.

Holy Spirit Prompts is for catching those in the moment, then letting them sit. Capture a prompting in a few words, a photo, a video, or your own voice. Tag it, note the verse it came with, and come back to it when something connects.

Promptings

A prompting is something sensed, and then tracked to an outcome.

  • Capture it however it comes — type a note, take a photo, film a video, record your voice, or import audio you already have.
  • Transcribe a recording on your device, so you can find what you said months later.
  • Track it as Active, Future, Fulfilled or Discarded, with a note for when it comes to pass.
  • Scripture links itself — type a reference and it becomes a way into every other prompting that shares the passage. Mention a verse in a note and the app quietly offers it.
  • See what connects — each prompting surfaces others sharing its tags and verses, weighted so a shared verse counts above a shared tag. Something from two years ago surfaces as readily as last week's.

God stories

A separate tab, deliberately much simpler. A story is something that already happened and gets written down once: a date, a few sentences, and one photo if you want it. No tags, no status, nothing to track.

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no subscription, no server, no analytics, no ads.

The only two things that ever leave your device are a backup you export yourself, and a single recording you choose to send to Apple to be transcribed. Both take a deliberate action from you, and nothing else is ever transmitted.

Photos and videos are referenced from your own Photos library rather than duplicated. You can export everything to one file whenever you like, protected with a passphrase if it's going somewhere you don't control.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

On-device transcription

iOS requires both Siri and Dictation to be enabled for on-device transcription — not just Dictation. If you'd rather leave them off, the app offers to send that one recording to Apple instead.
